## KEY OBSERVATIONS FOR MUDFISH DEVELOPMENT

1. **Chine 2 crosses DWL** — in the heights table, Chine 2 reads positive (above DWL) 
   at forward stations and negative (below DWL) at aft stations. 
   Crossing point approximately at station 4-5. Visible in both profile AND half-breadth views.

2. **Three chines** — CHINE 1, CHINE 2, KEEL/CHINE 3 — confirms the multi-card stack architecture

3. **Sheer has visible rise** — from 2-7-7 at station 10 rising to 3-2-6 at FP — 
   approximately 165mm of sheer rise bow to stern. Matches the FB_Min/FB_Bow formula.

4. **Keel has rocker** — KEEL/CHINE 3 absent at FP (stem closes), 
   rising from DWL at station T to 1-0-0 at station 5 (max depth at midship area)

5. **Station spacing 2'4¼"** = 711mm — 10 stations across LWL of 25'6½" = 7772mm

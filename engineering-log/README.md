# Engineering Log

This is the scaffolding — a running, dated record of *why* Mudfish is designed the way it is, not just *what* the final design turns out to be.

Most open-source hardware projects hand you a finished file and nothing else. This log exists so a builder — or a critic — can trace the reasoning behind every major decision: the question being asked, the formula or method used to answer it, the tradeoffs weighed, and where relevant, how AI-assisted exploration contributed to getting there. Nothing here is meant to be a black box.

## Format

Each entry is a dated Markdown file: `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`, containing:

- **The question** — what was being decided
- **The method** — formulas, references, calculations, or reasoning used
- **AI involvement** (if any) — what was explored with AI assistance, and what was independently verified
- **The conclusion** — what was decided and why
- **Open questions** — anything still unresolved, for future revisiting or community input
